## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** open any piece to see its photos, condition, measurements and where to buy it, and to browse by brand, **so that** I can decide on a piece and go buy it on Vinted or eBay.

<objective>
Finish the item page: the photo gallery with thumbnails, swipe and dots, the lightbox, the brand link and the "More from this brand" row.

Purpose: photos decide a pre-loved purchase; the row keeps the shopper in the shop. The only genuinely new client logic of the phase lives here (current-photo tracking and the dialog), kept in two small islands.
Output: ItemGallery and PhotoLightbox (client), MoreFromBrand (server), brandSlug, ROUTES.brand, the two-column item layout, and a mock item with three photos.
</objective>
