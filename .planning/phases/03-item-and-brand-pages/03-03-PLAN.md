## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** open any piece to see its photos, condition, measurements and where to buy it, and to browse by brand, **so that** I can decide on a piece and go buy it on Vinted or eBay.

<objective>
Ship browsing by brand: /brands/[slug] reuses the whole /shop catalogue with the brand locked, and /brands shows the in-stock brands as logo or name tiles. The header Brands dropdown moves to the brand pages.

Purpose: success criterion 5. One catalogue code path (D-13) means the brand page gets every Phase 2 behaviour (filters, sort, chips, paging, noindex rules, canonical) without a second copy to drift.
Output: a basePath threaded through the catalogue helpers and components, CataloguePage, the two brand routes, BrandGrid, the committed logo manifest with its sync test, and the repointed header.
</objective>
