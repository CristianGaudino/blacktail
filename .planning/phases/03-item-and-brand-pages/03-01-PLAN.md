## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** open any piece to see its photos, condition, measurements and where to buy it, and to browse by brand, **so that** I can decide on a piece and go buy it on Vinted or eBay.

<objective>
Ship the item page as a vertical slice: a shopper opens a card on /shop and reads the piece's brand, title, price, details, measurements and where to buy it; a gone piece is a real 404 with the newest drops, including right after the webhook for a piece that just sold. JSON-LD Product data rides on the page. The gallery and "More from this brand" land in Plan 02, the brand pages in Plan 03, the Worker proof in Plan 04.

Purpose: success criterion 3 (real 404 after the webhook) is the riskiest part of the phase, so it is proven first, end to end, on next start against the mock API, and the same checks run on workerd in Plan 04 through prove-freshness.
Output: getItem, /items/[code] with its gone page, the generic 404, the info components, the mock item route and four new prove-freshness checks.
</objective>
