## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** browse, filter, search, sort and page through the shop's in-stock pieces in the Blacktail Archive look on desktop and phone, **so that** I can quickly find pieces in my size, brand and budget.

<objective>
Put the Blacktail Archive look on the site and draw the template seam. After this plan `/shop` renders in Ivory and Forest with Bodoni Moda and Montserrat under an "All Items" banner, every shop-specific value lives in `src/shop/`, and a test fails if one leaks anywhere else.

Purpose: the design tokens, fonts, generic copy deck and shop seam are what Plans 03 to 06 style and read. Writing the whole phase's generic COPY here (from the UI-SPEC copywriting contract) is deliberate: Plans 03 and 04 run in parallel in wave 3 and would otherwise both edit src/lib/copy.ts.
Output: Tailwind 4 pipeline, `src/shop/` (config, colours, theme, fonts), CatalogueBanner, COPY deck with code-label maps, `label()`, the shop-string guard.

Task 1 is the package legitimacy checkpoint the research requires before any install (never auto-approved). Task 2 is the tracer: tokens and fonts reach the rendered /shop page. Task 3 completes the seam and the guard.

Scope rationale for Task 2 (14 files): four are font and licence files copied byte for byte and two are npm-generated, so six files are authored, each under about 40 lines.
</objective>
