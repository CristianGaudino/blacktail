## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** browse, filter, search, sort and page through the shop's in-stock pieces in the Blacktail Archive look on desktop and phone, **so that** I can quickly find pieces in my size, brand and budget.

<objective>
Put the Blacktail Archive look on the site. After this plan `/shop` renders in Ivory and Forest with Bodoni Moda and Montserrat under an "All Items" banner, and the theme and fonts live in `src/shop/`.

Purpose: the design tokens and fonts are what Plans 05 to 09 style with. Plan 03 then completes the shop seam (every other shop value, the COPY deck and the shop-string guard) and Plan 04 the pure money, date and paging rules; both start from this plan.
Output: Tailwind 4 pipeline, `src/shop/` (theme, fonts, a first config with name, locale and banner slot), CatalogueBanner.

Task 1 is the package legitimacy checkpoint the research requires before any install (never auto-approved). Task 2 is the tracer: tokens and fonts reach the rendered /shop page.

Scope rationale for Task 2 (14 files): four are font and licence files copied byte for byte and two are npm-generated, so six files are authored, each under about 40 lines.
</objective>
