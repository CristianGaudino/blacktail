## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** browse, filter, search, sort and page through the shop's in-stock pieces in the Blacktail Archive look on desktop and phone, **so that** I can quickly find pieces in my size, brand and budget.

<objective>
Turn the plain list into the mockup's card grid and add numbered pages. After this plan a shopper sees branded cards (photo, brand, title, colour and size, price in the shop's currency, a NEW badge on fresh pieces) that never shift while photos load, and can step through pages of 24.

Purpose: the card and pager are the core of All Items (CAT-01, CAT-07, CAT-10, SHELL-05). The loading skeleton changes how errors stream, so the Phase 1 outage proof is adjusted in the same plan.
Output: formatMoney, isNew, ItemCard, ItemGrid, paging helpers, Pager, /shop/loading.tsx, an outage proof that understands streaming.

Runs in wave 3 beside Plan 04 (header, footer, menu). The two share no file. Do not edit src/lib/copy.ts, src/app/layout.tsx or src/styles/globals.css here; read COPY.catalogue, COPY.pager and COPY.stock as written by Plan 02.
</objective>
