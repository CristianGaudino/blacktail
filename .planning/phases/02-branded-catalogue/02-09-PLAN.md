## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** browse, filter, search, sort and page through the shop's in-stock pieces in the Blacktail Archive look on desktop and phone, **so that** I can quickly find pieces in my size, brand and budget.

<objective>
Give shoppers the mockup's filter panel. After this plan a shopper ticks brands, categories, sizes, colours, era, condition, department and material, drags price and measurement sliders, and sees results update at once on desktop; on a phone the same panel opens in a drawer with an Apply button.

Purpose: CAT-02, CAT-03 and CAT-08 with decisions D-14 to D-20. Everything server-side (cleaning, chips, count, sort, pending state) already exists, so this plan is pure interaction on top of `shopHref`, `CatalogueShell` and `Sheet`.
Output: FilterPanel, FilterGroup/FacetList, ColourSwatches, RangeSlider, FilterDrawer, slider CSS.
</objective>
