## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** browse, filter, search, sort and page through the shop's in-stock pieces in the Blacktail Archive look on desktop and phone, **so that** I can quickly find pieces in my size, brand and budget.

<objective>
Give every page the mockup's header, footer and phone menu. After this plan a shopper can reach Shop types and in-stock brands from the header dropdowns, search from any page, find the shop's contact and Vinted and eBay profiles in the footer, and use the menu on a phone.

Purpose: SHELL-02, SHELL-03 and SHELL-04, with the "mock feel" the owner asked for (D-06, D-08): decorative bag, account, wishlist and a non-working newsletter box that never pretends to work.
Output: Header (+ NavDropdown, NavLink, HeaderSearch), Footer (+ SocialIcons), MobileMenu (+ Sheet, useModalDialog), ui buttons, utils, logo, restyled StatusScreen and error page.

Runs in wave 3 beside Plan 03. The two share no file: do not edit src/app/shop/*, src/components/catalogue/*, src/lib/config.ts or src/lib/copy.ts here (read COPY.nav, COPY.header, COPY.footer, COPY.socials, COPY.common and COPY.labels as written by Plan 02). All local proofs in this plan use ports 4011 (mock) and 3001 (next) so they never collide with Plan 03's 4010 and 3000.

Scope rationale for Task 1 (8 files): the four header components are each a small file and are only testable together through the rendered page; utils.ts and buttons.tsx are short copies from Vintage.
</objective>
