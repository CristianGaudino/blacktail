## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** land on a home page that matches the Blacktail Archive mockup, see the latest drops and read the shop's story and contact details, **so that** I trust the shop and come back for new pieces.

<objective>
Finish the home page with the two /filters-driven sections: the brand strip (in stock, then sold before even with no stock, then the default order, which only ranks) and the shop-by-category tiles that hide when their type has no stock. Both orderings are pure, tested functions in one module. A sold-before brand's page renders its empty catalogue instead of a 404 (D-21).

Purpose: BRAND-02, the remaining TMPL-01 items (default brand order and category tiles in the shop folder), and HOME-01 to HOME-03 for these two sections (D-05 to D-08, D-21).
