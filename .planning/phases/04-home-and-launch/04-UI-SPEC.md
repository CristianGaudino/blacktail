# Phase 04 - UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the Home and Launch phase. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Visual target: `docs/refs/just-dropped.png` (the home page: hero, brand strip, Just Dropped carousel, two promo banners, shop by category, story band; this is the reference per 04-CONTEXT) and the About page and phone mockups in `docs/refs/misc-mockup.png`. `docs/refs/homepage-mock.png` is the "Featured Finds" variant and is NOT the target (it only confirms hero, brand strip and promo-band styling). ## Color

Inherited: Dominant Ivory 60%, Secondary Sand / Stone / Olive 30%, Accent Forest 10%. No destructive colour (no destructive action in this phase).

| Role | Value | New usage in this phase |
|------|-------|-------------------------|
| Dominant (60%) | Ivory `#E8E5DD` | Page background, brand strip, carousel section, category section, contact panel, About text section |
| Secondary (30%) | Sand 40% (`#CBB89E` at 40%), Stone `#A69D8F`, Olive `#4A5A46` | Sand 40%: story band fill, photo frame behind a loading card image. Stone: 1px rules on the brand strip, carousel header rule, contact panel border and group rules, photo frame outline. Olive: strip label, carousel eyebrow, contact labels, About eyebrow, on Ivory only (never on the Sand story band) |
| Accent (10%) | Forest `#1F2A1F` | Listed below |

Accent reserved for (this phase, on top of the Phase 2 and 3 lists):
1. The hero overlay and its solid placeholder base (Forest 80% to 10% left to right on 768px and up; flat Forest 60% below 768px)
2. The two promo banners' overlay and placeholder base (same gradient, mirrored on the right banner)
3. The bottom gradient on each category tile (Forest 70% to 0%, bottom to top) behind its label
4. The Forest FILL of the one primary button, "Our story" on the story band
5. The Forest banner behind the Display title on `/just-dropped`, `/contact` (the existing `CatalogueBanner`) and the overlay of the About banner
6. The 2px keyboard focus ring (offset 4px) on every new interactive element

Not accent: the inverse (Ivory outline) buttons on photos, the brand strip, carousel arrows (Forest icons, no fill), the contact panel, links, headings.

Contrast rules for text on photos (HOME-03: the real photos are unknown, so the overlay carries the contrast):
- Every text block on a photo sits on at least Forest 60%. Worst case, a light Sand photo under 60% Forest still gives Ivory text about 4.9:1. The text block never extends past 50% of the hero width or 60% of a banner's width, which is where the gradient is still 60% or darker.
- Placeholder images are already dark (see Image slots), so the overlay never lightens them.
- Olive text is allowed only on Ivory. On Sand 40% and on every photo, text is Forest or Ivory. Stone is never used for text.

New button variant `inverse` (for text on photos): 1px Ivory border, Ivory text, no fill; hover fills Ivory at 10% over 150ms (none under reduced motion). The existing `primary` (Forest fill) is used only on the story band; the existing `secondary` (Forest border) on the contact platform links.

Brand logos in the strip are shown exactly as supplied with `mix-blend-multiply` (Phase 3 rule: never recolour, never `invert`).

Placeholder look (neutral, palette only, no text except the shop name on the OG image):
- Hero, `banner-*`: a soft diagonal gradient from Forest to Olive (no pattern), so the overlay still reads as dark.

## Layout and Interaction Contract

### Home (`/`; HOME-01, HOME-02, HOME-03, D-03 to D-08, D-12)

Primary focal point: the hero headline (the largest, highest-contrast element above the fold on every viewport). Secondary: the Just Dropped cards. The brand strip stays visually quiet (logos only, one hairline rule).

Section order (matches `just-dropped.png`): hero, brand strip, Just Dropped carousel, two promo banners, shop by category, story band, then the existing footer. Page background Ivory. One `<h1>` (the hero headline); every other section heading is an `<h2>`; each section is a `<section aria-labelledby>`.

Data reads: strip and tiles use `/filters`; carousel uses `/items` (newest first, 10). All go through the tagged client (so the webhook refreshes the home page). The page calls `await connection()` and has no `generateStaticParams` (established pattern). No `loading.tsx` on `/` (the page has no redirect and no 404, and a streamed skeleton would only add a flash over cached reads).

Data section failure (default, recorded): each of the three data sections reads inside its own try/catch and hides itself when its read fails, exactly like "More from this brand" in Phase 3. The static sections (hero, promo banners, story band) always render, so an API outage never leaves a blank page. The failure is logged, never shown as text, and the last good cache is kept (DATA-03).

#### Hero

- Full-bleed frame (420px / 480px), image `hero.jpg` with `fill`, `object-cover`, `priority`, `fetchPriority="high"`, `alt=""`.
- Overlay: flat Forest 60% below 768px; from 768px `bg-linear-to-r from-forest/80 via-forest/60 to-forest/10`. Text block as in Spacing, vertically centred.
- Eyebrow, headline (two sentences, one per line), a 1px 48px-wide Ivory rule (Ivory at 60%, decorative), then one `inverse` `ButtonLink` "Shop new arrivals" to `ROUTES.justDropped` with a trailing `ChevronRight` 16px, `min-w-48`, label left and icon right (`justify-between`).
- Static: no auto-play, no parallax, no animation.

#### Brand strip (BRAND-02, D-06 to D-08)

- Band with the Stone rules, then a flex row as in Spacing. The scrolling area is a `<ul>` with `overflow-x-auto`, `snap-x`, `snap-mandatory` (each cell `snap-start`), scrollbar hidden, `overscroll-x-contain`. It scrolls only inside its own frame; the page never scrolls sideways.
- The strip scrolls when the logos do not fit; when they fit they are left aligned with no empty scroll. There is no auto-scroll and no arrows (one-row scroll-snap, D-06). Keyboard users move through the logo links with Tab and the strip scrolls the focused logo into view.
- Cell: a link to `ROUTES.brand(slug)` (`prefetch={false}`), 112 x 44px, logo `next/image` with `fill`, `object-contain`, `mix-blend-multiply`, `unoptimized`, lazy, shown only when `BRAND_LOGOS` lists the slug (Phase 3 rule, same slug function). A brand without a logo shows its name as Bodoni 700 16px text, vertically centred, `whitespace-nowrap`, with 8px padding either side so the 44px hit area stays. Hover: the cell fills Sand 40% (150ms; none under reduced motion). Focus: the 2px ring on the cell.
- Order (D-07, one tested function; the UI only renders its output): brands in stock, then sold-before brands (the list is empty for now), then the default order from `src/shop/` (D-08, PENDING-OWNER). The strip shows brands that exist in `/filters` plus sold-before brands even with no stock (CONTEXT D-21; a sold-before brand page shows the empty catalogue, not a 404). The default order is a sort key, not a source of out-of-stock brands.
- Cap: the first 16 brands (so the DOM stays small); "View all brands" reaches the full Brands page.
- "View all brands": a link to `ROUTES.brands`. It stays outside the scrolling area so it is always visible.
- Zero brands in `/filters` (or a failed read): the whole band is hidden (HOME-02).

#### Just Dropped carousel (HOME-01, D-10, D-12)

- Header row as in Spacing. Heading and eyebrow as in Copywriting. Two `IconButton`s (`ChevronLeft`, `ChevronRight`) with `aria-label` "Previous items" and "Next items".
- Track: a `<ul>` of the existing `ItemCard`s (the cards are `<li>`, so the markup stays valid), `flex`, `overflow-x-auto`, `snap-x`, `snap-mandatory`, each card `shrink-0 snap-start` with the basis in Spacing, scrollbar hidden, `overscroll-x-contain`. The 10 newest in-stock pieces by `publishedAt`, no time window (D-10, D-12). The card is unchanged (NEW badge, heart, brand, title, colour and size, price); the New badge rule is 14 days (D-11).
- All cards lazy (none `eager`): the hero is the LCP image.
- Arrows scroll by the visible width (`scrollBy({ left: +-track.clientWidth })`, smooth, or instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`); the snap aligns the next card. The previous arrow is `disabled` at the start and the next arrow `disabled` at the end (50% opacity, no pointer), tracked on scroll (rAF-throttled) and by a `ResizeObserver`. No carousel library (D-12).
- Arrows are rendered only when the track overflows (items do not fit): with 1 or 2 items on a phone, 1 to 5 on a desktop, they are not shown. Before the script runs they are hidden, so a no-JS or fast-scroll visitor still sees a working swipeable track. With fewer items than the visible count the cards keep their normal width and sit left aligned (not stretched).
- Zero items (or a failed read): the whole section is hidden (HOME-02).
- Focusing a card with Tab scrolls it into view (native). The track itself is not a tab stop.
