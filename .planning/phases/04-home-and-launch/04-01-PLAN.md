## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want to** land on a home page that matches the Blacktail Archive mockup, see the latest drops and read the shop's story and contact details, **so that** I trust the shop and come back for new pieces.

<objective>
Lay the SEO and launch switch every later plan builds on: one validated env flag (`ALLOW_INDEXING`, off by default) drives robots.txt, the robots meta and the X-Robots-Tag header; a dynamic sitemap reads through the tagged client; and one `pageMetadata` helper gives every existing page its title, description, canonical and Open Graph tags. The new pages in Plans 02 to 04 use the same helper from birth.

Purpose: SEO-01 and SEO-03 (D-16, D-17, D-18). The four platform traps RESEARCH found (build-time header, shallow metadata merge, unescaped sitemap URLs, cached metadata routes) are settled first, on pages that already exist.
Output: src/lib/indexing.ts, the env flag, robots.ts, sitemap.ts with its pure builder, pageMetadata on /shop, /brands, /brands/[slug] and /items/[code], SHOP.seo copy, and the og-default.jpg placeholder.

Scope note: this plan touches 23 files, over the usual 5-per-task guide. The owner reviewed and accepted the file count on 2026-11-03 (keep 5 plans, do not split): most entries are colocated tests, one-line caller updates and a placeholder image, the plan is one vertical slice led by a tracer, and the estimate is under the 100k budget. This is a documented deviation, not an oversight.
</objective>
