## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want** a sold piece's address to keep working as a clear Sold page, and a piece that is for sale to send me to its exact Vinted or eBay listing, **so that** shared links never dead-end and I land on the right listing.

<objective>
Ship the Sold item page as a vertical slice. Today `getItem` turns a sold piece into a 404. After this plan a sold piece answers 200 with its photos, title and details, a "Sold on <date>" line and a sold panel in place of where-to-buy, with JSON-LD SoldOut and noindex. A hidden, deleted or unknown piece is still a real 404, including right after the webhook. The Sold badge on the cover and the unit-test edge cases are Plan 02; per-item listing buttons are Plan 03; the workerd proof and the phase PR are Plan 04.

Purpose: SOLD-01..04 and success criteria 1 to 3. The riskiest part is the status flip after the webhook (in stock to Sold 200, in stock to 404), so the tracer proves both on next start against the mock first; Task 2 then proves the sparse sold shape (no price, no date, no photos) on the running site. The same prove-freshness checks run on workerd in Plan 04.
Output: itemDetailSchema and ItemDetail, getItem without the sold-to-null mapping, formatDay, SoldNotice, the sold branch in the item page and its metadata, JSON-LD SoldOut, neutral 404 copy, a mock `hidden` set and a bare sold piece (MOCK-0031), and the prove-freshness item-sold, item-hidden and item-sold-bare checks.
</objective>
