## Phase Goal

**As a** shopper, **I want** a sold piece's address to keep working as a clear Sold page, and a piece that is for sale to send me to its exact Vinted or eBay listing, **so that** shared links never dead-end and I land on the right listing.

<objective>
Ship the Sold item page as a vertical slice. Today `getItem` turns a sold piece into a 404. After this plan a sold piece answers 200 with its photos, title and details, a "Sold on <date>" line, a Sold badge on the cover and a sold panel in place of where-to-buy, with JSON-LD SoldOut and noindex. A hidden, deleted or unknown piece is still a real 404, including right after the webhook. Per-item listing buttons are Plan 02; the workerd proof and the phase PR are Plan 03.

Purpose: SOLD-01..04 and success criteria 1 to 3. The riskiest part is the status flip after the webhook (in stock to Sold 200, in stock to 404), so the tracer proves both on next start against the mock first. The same prove-freshness checks then run on workerd in Plan 03.
Output: itemDetailSchema and ItemDetail, getItem without the sold-to-null mapping, formatDay, SoldNotice, the sold branch in the item page and its metadata, JSON-LD SoldOut, the cover badge, neutral 404 copy, a mock `hidden` set, and the prove-freshness item-sold and item-hidden checks.
</objective>
